## Subscriptions and free trials

EasyBurn requires an active subscription or an eligible Apple free trial after
setup. Yearly and Monthly unlock the same features. The app shows Apple's
localized price and any eligible trial before purchase; Yearly is billed
annually, even when a monthly equivalent is shown.

Manage or cancel a subscription in EasyBurn's Profile, or in **iPhone Settings →
your name → Subscriptions**. You can also
[open Apple's subscription management](https://apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions).
Follow Apple's displayed cancellation deadline, including any deadline before a
free trial ends. Deleting the app or using Start Over does not cancel a
subscription. Trial reminders depend on notification permission and do not
cancel the trial for you.

If you already subscribed, use **Restore Purchases** on the paywall while signed
in to the Apple Account used for the purchase. Contact Apple to
[request a refund](https://reportaproblem.apple.com/); refunds are handled by
Apple.

## Food and workout estimates and barcodes

Food and workout estimates are approximate. Describe your food with portions, or
your workout with duration and intensity, and choose **Estimate**. Review and edit
the result before choosing **Add to log**. The server interprets workouts; their
calories are calculated on your iPhone using your weight. Health records, body
measurements and weight history are not sent to the estimation service. An
online estimate needs a connection and an active subscription. If a request
fails, your draft stays available; you can enter calories manually or use a
clearly labelled built-in estimate when offered for recognized foods.

Barcode lookup uses Open Food Facts. If a product is missing or its information
is inaccurate, enter the calories from its package and review the amount before
saving. Camera and Photos permissions can be changed in iPhone Settings.

## Apple Health and reminders

Health access is optional and read-only. Check the data you share with EasyBurn
in Apple Health or iPhone Settings. Health activity contributes to a date's
totals only after you add a manual food, calorie, photo or workout entry on that
date. A day without food does not have a calculated deficit.

Change meal-reminder times in Profile's Reminders. If reminders are not
arriving, check EasyBurn's notification permission and your iPhone's Focus
settings.

## Exporting, restoring or deleting your log

Your log is only on your iPhone and is excluded from device and iCloud backups.
Use **Profile → Export Data** to save a copy. Export remains available on the
paywall without an active subscription. Photos are not included in exports.

**Profile → Import data** restores an export after confirmation and replaces the
current local log. An import does not restore a subscription; use Restore
Purchases for that. Profile may offer Recover earlier data if a readable
recovery copy exists. Start Over deletes the local log and photos. We do not
hold a cloud copy of your diary to restore for you.

## Estimates and safety

EasyBurn is for adults and provides general calorie and weight estimates, not
medical advice. Food and workout estimates, daily burn, estimated loss and goal dates can be
inaccurate. Check with a doctor before changing your diet or exercise,
particularly if you have a health condition.
