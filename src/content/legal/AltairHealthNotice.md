## General wellness

Altair is a general wellness app for reviewing activity, sleep, meals, and other
recorded health information. Its
reports, scores, AI conversations, and nutrition estimates are for general
wellness information. Altair does not provide medical advice, diagnosis,
treatment, or prescriptions. It does not replace care from a qualified
healthcare professional.

## Estimates have limits

Imported measurements may be incomplete, delayed, or inaccurate. A score
reflects the information and reference values available to the app; it does not
establish that you are healthy or rule out a medical problem. AI can provide
incorrect or misleading answers. Food photos cannot reliably identify every
ingredient, allergen, portion size, or nutrient.

Do not use Altair to decide medication doses, determine whether a food is safe
for an allergy, or make treatment decisions. Discuss medical questions and
significant changes to diet or activity with a qualified healthcare
professional, especially if you have a medical condition.

## Emergencies

Altair does not continuously monitor your health, provide emergency alerts, or
contact a clinician. If you think you may have a medical emergency, contact your
local emergency services directly. Do not wait for a response from Altair.
