_Last updated: 9 July 2026_

This Privacy Policy explains how the Floyd app ("Floyd", "we", "us") collects,
uses, and protects your information. Floyd is an AI-powered calendar developed by
Emir Yorulmaz (untreu). By using Floyd you agree to the practices described here.

## Information We Collect

**Account information.** When you sign in with Apple, we receive a unique
account identifier and, if you choose to share it, your name and email address.
We use this solely to create and secure your account.

**Calendar events.** Events you create in Floyd, or import from Apple Calendar,
are stored so we can sync them across your devices and display them to you. Floyd
only reads your Apple Calendar when you explicitly choose to import events.

**Assistant messages.** When you use Floyd's AI assistant, the text (or
transcribed voice input) you send is processed to generate a response. Requests
are relayed through our server to a third-party AI provider (OpenRouter) to
produce the response.

**Profile picture.** If you set a profile picture, the image you choose is stored
on our server and associated with your account.

**Voice input.** If you use voice dictation, speech is transcribed to text on
your device by Apple's on-device speech recognition. We do not store raw audio.

## How We Use Your Information

We use the information above to:

- Provide, operate, and sync the Floyd calendar and assistant.
- Authenticate you and keep your account secure.
- Process your subscription and enforce usage limits.
- Send you event reminder notifications, if you enable them.
- Diagnose problems and improve the app.

We do **not** sell your personal information, and we do **not** use your calendar
events or assistant messages to serve advertising.

## Subscriptions

Floyd offers an optional auto-renewable subscription, **Floyd Pro Monthly**.
Purchases and renewals are handled by Apple through your Apple Account. To verify
your subscription, Apple provides us a signed transaction that we validate on our
server to unlock Pro features. We do not receive or store your payment card
details — those are handled entirely by Apple.

## Third-Party Services

Floyd relies on the following third parties to function:

- **Apple** — Sign in with Apple, in-app purchases, push notifications, and
  on-device speech recognition.
- **Firebase (Google)** — authentication and account management.
- **OpenRouter** — processes assistant requests to generate AI responses.

Each of these services processes data under its own privacy policy.

## Data Retention

We keep your account data, calendar events, and profile picture for as long as
your account is active. Assistant messages are processed to generate responses
and are not retained for advertising or profiling. You may delete your profile
picture at any time from within the app.

## Your Choices

- **Notifications.** You can enable or disable event reminders in Settings, and
  in the iOS system settings.
- **Calendar access.** You control whether Floyd may import from Apple Calendar
  via the iOS permission prompt.
- **Profile picture.** You can change or remove it at any time.
- **Account deletion.** To request deletion of your account and associated data,
  contact us using the details below.

## Children's Privacy

Floyd is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect
personal information from children.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be
reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
