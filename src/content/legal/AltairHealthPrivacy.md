_Draft for launch review · Prepared 15 September 2026_

This separate Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy describes Altair’s collection
and sharing of consumer health data. Altair is a general wellness app developed by Emir Yorulmaz
(untreu). This draft requires final operator, processor, and request-contact
details before publication as a launch policy.

## Categories collected and purposes

- **Health and fitness measurements:** activity, energy, exercise, heart and
  respiratory readings, other permitted vitals, body measurements, mobility,
  nutrition, sleep intervals, and workout summaries. Altair synchronizes approved
  categories to its server and uses them for history, reports, and insights.
- **Health profile and diet:** age, sex, height, weight, dietary preferences,
  restrictions, allergies, meal descriptions and photos, eating times, and
  estimated calories and nutrients. These support personal context and the meal
  journal. Some preferences may also reveal religious beliefs.
- **Health communications:** health information in messages, submitted photos,
  voice audio, transcripts, assistant instructions, and enabled saved memories.
  These support conversations, meal actions, transcription, and spoken replies.
- **Inferences and associated identifiers:** reports, scores, nutrition
  estimates, and health context inferred from the above, associated with an
  account identifier and relevant dates and times. These support the requested
  wellness features and synchronization.

Altair does not read workout routes or precise location from Apple Health. It
does not write information back to Apple Health. Not every category is present
for every person; collection depends on permissions, available records, and the
information submitted.

## Sources

Health data comes from you; Apple Health records you authorize, which may
originate from your iPhone, Apple Watch, or connected apps and devices; and
Altair’s calculations and AI-generated inferences based on that information.

## Categories shared, recipients, and purposes

- **Hosting and database service providers** process the above account-linked
  health content to operate Altair’s server and storage. The hosting operator
  and processing regions must be identified before launch.
- **OpenRouter and serving AI model providers** process health summaries,
  relevant profile and meal context, conversation text, instructions, enabled
  memories, and submitted photos to answer questions and analyze or manage meals.
  Google Gemini is the configured default model family; the final provider list
  and routing restrictions must be confirmed before launch.
- **ElevenLabs** processes microphone audio for transcription and reply text for
  speech. These can contain the health information you discuss and information
  reflected in a reply. Transcribed text is used in the AI flow above.

Altair does not sell consumer health data or use it for advertising. There is no
affiliate-sharing integration in the current service. Subscription requests to
Apple and RevenueCat do not include health measurements or conversation content.

## Exercising your rights

You can review or revoke Apple Health permissions, review or disable assistant
memory, delete saved memories, edit or delete meals, and delete your Altair
account in Settings. Removing Health permissions does not erase existing
server data; deleting a memory does not erase its source conversation.

Applicable consumer health laws may provide rights to confirm collection,
access health data and information about recipients, withdraw collection or
sharing consent, and request deletion, including downstream deletion where
required. The private request address, identity-verification process, and appeal
channel are pending launch review and must be supplied before release. Public
social profiles and GitHub issues must not be used to submit health data.
