## Signing in

Choose your country code and enter your phone number to request a verification
code. Check that the number is correct and can receive SMS. If the message is
delayed, wait before requesting another code. Contact support if you still
cannot sign in; never share your verification code with us.

## Apple Health and missing data

Altair can read only the Apple Health categories you allow. Review Altair's
permissions in the Health app, and check that your iPhone or Apple Watch has
recorded the information you expect. Some summaries need more history before
they can be shown. Altair's health profile, meals, reports and conversations
are stored on your iPhone. Deleting the app or changing devices does not
restore that history, so do not reinstall the app as a troubleshooting step.

## Altair+ subscriptions

Altair+ is a monthly subscription. The app shows the current local price before
you confirm a purchase with Apple. To restore an existing purchase, sign in to
Altair and use **Restore purchases** in the membership screen with the Apple
Account used for the original purchase. If access does not return, contact
support with the error message; do not send payment-card information.

Manage or cancel your subscription in **iPhone Settings → your name →
Subscriptions → Altair**. Deleting Altair or your Altair account does not cancel
the subscription. For billing or refund requests, visit
[Apple's purchase support](https://reportaproblem.apple.com/).

## AI, voice and privacy

Cloud AI and voice are optional and require separate consent. You can change
your choices in **Settings → Data & privacy → Privacy & health**. Voice also
requires microphone permission and an internet connection. In **Assistant
memory**, turn memory off to stop its use or choose **Clear memory** to remove
saved notes.

To delete your account, use **Settings → Account**. Account deletion is also
available from the Account menu during setup. For privacy, access or deletion
requests, email [contact@dame.dev](mailto:contact@dame.dev).

Altair provides general wellness information and estimates. It does not provide
medical advice, diagnosis, treatment or emergency monitoring. For a medical
emergency, contact local emergency services directly.
