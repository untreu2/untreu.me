## Subscriptions

EasyBurn requires an active auto-renewable subscription, or an eligible Apple
free trial, after setup. Yearly and Monthly unlock the same features. The app
shows Apple's localized price, billing period and any eligible trial before you
confirm a purchase with Apple.

Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase, or when
an eligible free trial ends. Subscriptions renew automatically unless you
cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel
in **iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions**. Deleting the app or using
Start Over does not cancel a subscription. Refunds are handled by Apple.

## Estimates are not medical advice

EasyBurn is for adults aged 18 or older. It provides general calorie and weight
estimates, not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Food estimates, daily
burn, estimated loss and goal dates can be inaccurate. Review food estimates
before adding them, and check with a doctor before changing your diet or
exercise, particularly if you have a health condition.
